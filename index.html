Lavender Corazón

A Queer & Latine Press

Lavender Corazón Press is a small press rooted in queer and Latine experiences. We make experimental artists' books using techniques and aesthetics drawn from fine press traditions. As a printing method, letterpress has long been inaccessible to our communities owing to costly equipment, specialized apprenticeship, and limited studio access. We use the medium to challenge historical notions about whose voices are accepted as worthy of literary weight.

For inquiries about our work, collaborations, or other questions, please reach out.
